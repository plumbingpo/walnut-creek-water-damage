Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Day or night, 24/7, Walnut Creek Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Walnut Creek to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Walnut Creek

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.
