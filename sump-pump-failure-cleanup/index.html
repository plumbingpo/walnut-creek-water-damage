Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Help in Walnut Creek

The moment a basement most needs its sump pump is often the moment the pump gives out. During heavy rain the water table climbs and fills the pit, at which point an outage, a stuck float or an overwhelmed pump stops the pumping. Water rises over the rim of the pit and spreads across the basement floor. Until pumping resumes, the water continues to rise.

Clean is the wrong word for what comes out of a sump pit. Soil, silt, bacteria and the contents of the drain tile travel with it, so our team rates it no better than Category 2, and Category 3 when it has stood for a while or sewage is involved. That changes the cleanup: porous materials like carpet pad and wet drywall usually come out, and everything left is cleaned and disinfected before drying starts.

Right Now

Your First Steps

  1. Stay Out of the WaterDo not step into a flooded basement while the power is on. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Turn Off the Basement CircuitsTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. Call the utility when the panel itself sits in the flooded area.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceSee if the plug pulled out, the outlet has tripped or the float got stuck on the side of the pit. Do not reach into the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyWear rubber boots and gloves if you must go down, keep kids and pets upstairs, and wash your hands after touching anything wet.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallTake pictures of the pump and the water level, bring valuables that are still dry upstairs, and phone us. Holding off until the basement empties is not the answer.
How We Fix It

What Happens After You Call

1

Pump Out the Basement

We bring our own pumps and extractors, so nothing depends on your failed sump. We clear silt and standing water off the floor, out of low areas and from around the pit.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Wash and Disinfect

We wash the framing, the slab and the contents that are staying, then apply an antimicrobial. Doing it ahead of drying keeps the air movers from spreading contaminants through the basement.

4

Dry and Confirm

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. A working pump should be back in the pit before our equipment leaves.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • A pump too small for the volume of water entering the pit.
  • Discharge piping that has come apart, been flattened or plugged up outdoors.
  • Stuck or snagged float switches, which never tell the pump to start.
  • Years of on and off cycling finally burning out an aging motor.
  • A power outage during the storm with no battery backup on the pump.
If It Is Left

What the Water Damages

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Dirty water climbs from the slab into insulation and drywall, far higher than the line you can see. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Carpet and Flooring

There is no safe way to clean pad and carpet that sat in groundwater, so both are removed. Vinyl plank and laminate floors generally have to be pulled as well, since dirty water stays under them.

Basement Mechanical Equipment

The furnace and water heater frequently sit on the same floor as the pit. Controls, wiring and burners can be destroyed by water, so every appliance needs an inspection before it runs again.

Outlets and Wiring

Wall outlets near the floor are sometimes submerged in a sump flood. Have an electrician look at wet receptacles and cable before power is restored to them.

Coverage

Does Insurance Cover It?

Sump pump losses are widely misunderstood when it comes to insurance. Water backing up or overflowing from a sump is usually excluded under a standard homeowners policy, and a flood policy is a different product with rules of its own. Often the only coverage comes from an added endorsement for water backup and sump overflow, which has limits of its own. Look for that endorsement on your declarations page or ask your agent. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.
