Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Walnut Creek, Walnut Creek Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
